Dr. Hugo G. Vivien

Hello, I'm an astrophysicist and deep learning expert working as a postdoctoral researcher at the Laboratoire d'Astrophysique de Marseille.

I work on ESA's PLATO mission by working on the statistical validation, or vetting, of planet candidates in its data reduction pipeline. We are developing the validation algorithms, and I am implementing them in a prototype to assess their performance.

A little more
about me.

I have been fascinated by space ever since I first watched videos of rockets taking off. What began as an obsession with rocket engineering slowly transformed into an interest for space in general.

I studied physics and chemistry before earning master's degrees in astrophysics and business intelligence at Aix-Marseille University. In 2025, I completed my Ph.D. in astrophysics focused on exoplanet research.
